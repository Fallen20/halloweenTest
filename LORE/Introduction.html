You approach the giant mansion you heard so many horror stories about. The name makes you chill as you read it on the plate near a brick pillar: The Scarlet Nest.
It lives up to its name, as the color of the bricks is more red than regular bricks you’ve seen in your life, as somehow they’re partially made of bloods of the unlucky people and pokemon that entered in the past years. You are skeptical to believe a 1k year ghost still roames inside, hoping to catch new souls to make them its servants but, again, you live in a world where humans have done this for many years, and, hell, ghost pokemons that had lived on for centuries are still here!

You search in your pocket for the paper that was sent to you specifically in the past week and made you come here, despite all the fear and regret you’re feeling right now. The letter is very wrinkled and balled up in the deepest pocket you could have, the paper feeling almost like a leaf in texture of how many times you had opened and closed it again. How many times did you read it, thinking it was just a joke until you decided to give it a try? At this point, you already know each word by memory.

“Welcome fellow friend!
I am pleased to invite you to my mansion, the so-called haunted mansion! Yes, you’re reading right, the Scarlet Nest still has habitants in it despite all the rumors! Wealth is something you can’t really say no when you win so much, don’t you agree?
I wait for you in this direction, I even included a map for you, am I nice? I am, right? Don’t be late, the star of the party is you!“

The map you received is long gone; who couldn’t know where the famous mansion was? And the writer thought they were being nice? They were surely beyond mad, but since you were here, weren’t you as mad as them?

You turned the letter into a ball again as you put it in the same pocket as you try to move the gate fence. It just rattles at your pushing, not wanting to open at your strength. If it was like the letter said, someone lived here and obviously they didn’t want others to enter without permission.
You look around the fence, somehow expecting to find a rock with a key under that will magically open the giant gate. To find a secret button that will open a secret entrance, you’ll need to crawl under it and enter from the garden. Or with enough will the doors will open.
Or maybe you’ve seen too many detective and mystery movies and believed these things could happen in real life when, in fact, you ended in the same way as before you tried to push the doors.

But as you are in your misery, a loud, cheerful laugh comes from below the plate with the name. Below it, way too far from your reach, there’s a small, almost invisible speaker, lacking everything except the part with the dots.
“Look how’s here! Miss or mister detective thinks there’s a secret to open the door! AHAHAHA!”. The voice continued to mock you between laughs for long minutes, which made you think that being here was a real waste of time. “Sorry young one, this oh so mysterious mansion only has an old, simple doorbell. Even if it was too hard to find for you, let me give you your reward for making me see the energy of someone impatient to enter. Welcome to the Scarlet Nest!”

The gate cracked open slowly. In a matter of a second you could already pass through the massive gate, but continued to open fully. You think that if you came with a truck, you could still have enough space to enter, even with 2 or 3.
Careful, you look around to see a big garden; it’s almost like you’re inside a movie. There’s crimson roses with black thorns all around, with tall trees you can only describe as related to pines in some way or another, as they have the same shape of leaves. The grass was decorated here and there with clovers and warm grey rocks and in the middle, on where you were standing, a path of smooth stones leading to the entrance. You walk slowly, watching over somehow expecting to see a crow or suddenly something that would let you turn back with a reason, but as you are in front of the door, nothing has happened. The gate has long closed, telling you there was no escape until the supposed party ended. Gold decorations surround the door frame in what should be a language, but you can’t make any words. If you squint your eyes enough, you can recognize some letters, but they turn out to be the same random strokes once you open your eyes fully.
Learning from the last time, you easily see a doorbell and you press it gently. Despite being gentle, a loud bell sound resonates all around as a laugh that could only be described as cold echoes all around as the door opens.

A red carpet laid across the open door, a long hallway with a dark brown door at the end, with no other door around the solitary yet illuminated mansion. You take a step in and approach the room after inspecting all the places as the door behind you remains open. The dark oak door seems to be something out of place, way too simple when all you saw was luxury. As you turn the knock, you see a squareish room with three doors of the same material and color as the one you’re opening and before you can process what you’re seeing, a hard sound echoes in your ears. You turn around to see what it is.

The door to the outside is closed. A loud laugh you remember as the one in the intercom sounds all around without you being able to see any visible speaker.

“Oh my, aren’t you a little brave avocado. Let me introduce myself first, of course. I’m Sangría, the proud owner of this mansion, as you can imagine. Or not, the first impression you gave me wasn’t of the brightest all around.” You suppress a growl as before you can even reply the voice- Sangria’s- continues to talk.

“Welcome to my little party, poor soul. You aren’t the first to enter and probably the last. A cycle has started where you will be forced to try to resolve everything to try to exit”. You can see that she has really emphasized the word ‘try’.

“Worry not, because you’re not the first, maybe you can see the trail of clues the fallen heroes left for the poor souls beyond their help. Mind you, I’ll also give you a clue! After all, I liked your first impression. Open your ears, okay? I won’t repeat it". You brace yourself, trying to calm your heart to listen as much as possible.

“Remember where you came from. Remember the date. Was that clear? Were all of you listening?” She talked like there was someone else, but you can’t recall seeing anyone. “Welcome to the Scarlet Nest, where the only party will be red! I wish you all the luck, I am rooting for you”

With this, the voice shut up. No more laughs, no more static or something. There was only silence and you in this room.
