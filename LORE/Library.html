Swiftly writing the password with little to no error, the shelf reveals a path behind as you enter, the shelf not having any intention of closing.
Before you enter, you see a book out of the shelf that you don’t recall it was like this. You go to put it correctly again as you step on a card with the letter S engraved on it. You take the card and put the book where and how it should as you step inside, walking across the corridor.

As you approach, a small tablet on the wall illuminates, showing questions. If you have to guess,the door will open when you answer the questions.
