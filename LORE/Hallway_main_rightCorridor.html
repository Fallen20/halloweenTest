You turn to the other corridor as the first turn happens immediately. You expect another eternal corridor and, in fact, you’re right.
The first part seemed to be just a fake sensation to make you believe you won’t be more than 10 minutes walking. If it wasn’t because you came from a hallway almost the same size (or what you think they are the same size), you would have given up mid way. At least, this one doesn’t look as creepy as the other as every inch of it has light, not a single shade of darkness visible at sight.

As you continue to walk, expect to see something similar as the previous long walk but the hallway is very empty. Time to time you see some fancy lanterns but not a single thing that catches your attention.
After a long time, you can see a door and you rush to it, opening with so much happiness you almost forget you are trapped in this place.
