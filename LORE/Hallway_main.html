You follow the hallway decorated with sparse lights all around the walls. Now you walk along it, the further you go the more decorated the walls become.
There are some portraits with what you suppose are the previous owners of this mansion, all of them having a golden plate below with the name and a date. It is the birth and death date, maybe?

There’s many portaits with different painting ways: from simple to more detailed, even one so abstract you don’t know what you’re supposed to see. Most of them, especially the first ones, are only humans you can only describe as people living in the Middle age, with shining silver armor, battle-hardened faces.
As more appear, they start to go missing: some of them are ripped out, others are directly missing, only the plate and the frame telling you there’s supposed to be something there.
People in it seem to be more and more relaxed in what came to battle themes as the corridor starts to end. The battle armor that covered most of the face was now replaced with jewels or family portraits with much more color and expressivity than the serious look from the previous ones. The background started to shift from a boring color to have proper furniture till, in one portrait, you stop.

There’s something different in this one.
Maybe the color? No, it seems the same vibrant as before.
Maybe you know someone? No, that would be dumb. The place says it’s from ages ago, you wonder if your family name was even created back then. There’s only… 3 people in it. 3 people.
Maybe.. the placement? It was alone in the wall, the next one too far.

But that was only it. Maybe the owner wanted to highlight it for some reason and that’s what is making you think differently?

You continue to walk as you give a last look to it.

As you arrive at the end of the corridor, you see 3 options presented in front of you:
A giant door without a knock or anything. You push it and hit it with all your might but it doesn't move. Despite that, you can clearly see that there are, in fact, 2 doors so there should be a way to open it, right?

Just nexto the door, there’s a panel, the same panel with red light that you could barely see from the previous room. The panel has 10 numbers, from 0 to 9 with a text on top. ‘Remember where you came from’. You are surprised to see that is the same clue the owner of the mansion gave you. Paying attention to her wasn’t important after all…
You input random numbers in it but it doesn't seem to work, but you continue to try. Seems the answer is 4 numbers

On your right, there’s another long corridor. But unlike the one you came from, you can see that it turns left just a couple of meters away. You can’t see too much but there’s enough light to see everything so maybe you can continue.

You always have the option to go back, maybe some of the room has a clue of how to escape or what to do.
