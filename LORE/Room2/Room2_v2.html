As you move the last piece, the portrait starts to move along with the wall. It soon disappears but the full portrait, without a missing piece, suddenly appears from the wall from your right, where the wall had been going. You can see now there’s a small letter C where the missing piece was as you take a mental note of it.

You continue to go throught the corridor, this time seemingly to be shorter. At the end, an elegant door with a knock awaits you as you open and appear in a new room.
The room barely has anything, just a shelf with books of tales fills it, but none of them seems too important for you to read all of them carefully.

At your right there’s a door or more like it, the frame of a door. Like the first room, this one is open but the walls are completely white with only a few spots you can spot from where you’re standing.

At your left, there’s an actual door, closed. You leave the door you came in open to try this new door as you notice that it can easly open, leading to a long corridor. Safe enough, you think. You recall seeing this exact corridor in the first room.

As you turned around, you heard a door close as the one you came in just closed without you even touching it. You approach but there’s nothing to grab, despite you knowing that it can be opened. Despite all your will to try to reopen the door, you can’t open it and you’re left in the room.

There’s nothing more to do with it. It’s you and the two doors.
