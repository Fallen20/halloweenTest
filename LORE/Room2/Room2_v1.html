You open the door rushing, a simple knock to push reveals you a bigger room than the first one.
The room is barely filled with objects, a simple shelf with books of tales fills it, but none of them seems too important for you to read all of them carefully.

On the right side, there’s a door with white sparkles, could it signify stars? You notice there’s no knock or anything that allows you to open it normally. You push it, slam it and even kick it but doesn’t even show any movement or will to open. Guess it’s closed from this side.

In front of you, there’s another door, the last one. Like the previous room, this one is also open but the walls are completely white with only a few spots you can spot from where you’re standing.

There’s nothing more; not a secret path, not a button to push and go out either. It’s you, this single door you can go throught or return.
