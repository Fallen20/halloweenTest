As you put the last letter in the correct order, secret stairs appear. You start to climb it as you see another door at the end. You're getting quite tired of them.

Despite that, you open it and you find yourself in one of those fancy dining rooms where the room is way too long with an even longer table. Said table is way too decorated, as somehow it's prepared for a feast.
As you take a step in, the door closes with a hard hit as laughs start to fill the room. The chairs move as someone is sitting to eat and you're quite sceptical to even take a seat so you stay where you are, frozen. >

You hear a familiar voice as you can see one ghost; at this moment you are not even sure if it's a pokemon or something else. It looks similar to a weavile but her colors remind you of a ghost pokemon. The ghostly flames around her only makes you believe it more.

"Congratulations for being the first ever escape! Many had tried without success." Despite not laughing, you can see a smile on her face. She was having too much fun. "As for your prize? Oh don't worry, you earned it" She snapped her claw-like fingers as the note you've already forgotten goes out of your pocket and gently floats in front of you.

"Here, an invitation! I look forward to seeing you here next year" You can't stay shut this time as you refuse her invitation but she never loses her smile. " Oh, you don't understand. It's an invitation you can't refuse "
She suddenly disappeared and in a blink of an eye, she's in front of you. She's way taller than you and pushes the invitation with her claw to your chest.

"Now, since you're so eager to go this year, why won't we leave the celebration for next year? I'm sure more people will come. The more the merrier, as you all say"

You couldn't reply as suddenly dizziness fills you.

>When you open your eyes, you're in front of the gate to the Scarlet Nest. Did you faint? You get up somehow as you touch the gate, suddenly the rusty sensation filling your hand. You look at it, ready to go back and ask for an explanation as you notice the gate has way too many padlocks with giant, heavy chains. They all seem rusted, like they've been here for so many years. But there's no way, you just were inside and there was no chain!
You search for the bell but the place where it was now is filled with vines and dead leaves. As you click,nothing comes from it. Not a laugh, not a single sound.

You take a step back, you can see that the mansion you saw it's nothing like before: the scarlet bricks are now grey, with cracks all around. The tops of the pillars are decorated with vines and dead leaves, nothing ressembling the place you've been in. The bushes and trees are either dead or have been cut, leaving a stump.
All looks abandoned and not only for a few hours but way too many years. But that's not possible. You clearly remember all you've been throught, all the solutions so it can't be.
Or maybe you have been daydreaming about what could have happened if the letter was real. You are way too imaginative, your friends have said it too many times. Was there something that could prove it was not a dream?

You put your hand in your pockets as you walk in circles, unable to stay still. You find the invitation as you take it out.

It's golden, with not a single sign of being treated like trash but nicely folded. Inside, the same words you remember: 'Invitation for next year'

It was not a dream and there was no escape.
